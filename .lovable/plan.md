# Naughty words score double

Rude words become the best plays on the board: any word on a rude-word list scores double, and it gets a pink outline so you can see why.

## Where the list comes from

Public, freely reusable source: the widely used "List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene and Otherwise Bad Words" (English), plus a small hand-added set of anatomical and slang terms it misses (clit, vagina, penis, bukkake, etc.). Only entries that actually exist in the official tournament word list already loaded in the app are kept, so nothing invalid becomes playable. The result is saved as a file shipped with the app.

## How it plays

- Every game, always on. No setting to turn it off.
- When a move forms a word on the rude list, that word's points are doubled (after normal letter and square bonuses). Other words formed in the same move score normally.
- The move preview badge shows the doubled total, so you see the reward before committing.
- Tiles belonging to a rude word get a pink outline on the board — both while previewing and once played.
- The move history marks the word, e.g. "CUNT (2x)".

## Technical notes

- New `public/naughty-words.txt`, generated by intersecting the public list with `public/words.txt`; loaded lazily alongside the dictionary in `src/lib/words.ts` as a second cached `Set`.
- `validateMove` in `src/lib/scrabble.ts` takes an optional `isNaughty` predicate; each entry in `result.words` gains a `naughty` flag and doubled score; `result.score` sums the doubled values. Bingo bonus stays applied once at the end.
- `GameState.board` tiles gain an optional `naughty` marker set when a rude word is played, so the pink outline survives reloads; `Board.tsx` renders a pink ring for those cells and for pending tiles in a rude word.
- Pink is added as a design token in `src/styles.css` (no hardcoded colours).
